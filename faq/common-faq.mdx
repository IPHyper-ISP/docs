  <Accordion title="**What is a static residential proxy?**">
    A static residential proxy is a proxy service that uses IP addresses from residential networks. These IP addresses typically remain unchanged throughout the subscription period. It is ideal for cross-border e-commerce, social media management, digital advertising, and other use cases that require a consistent IP address over time.
  </Accordion>

  </Accordion>

    IPHyper's Standard and Custom ISP Proxies support both HTTP and SOCKS5 protocols. You can choose the appropriate protocol based on your software, device, and business requirements.
