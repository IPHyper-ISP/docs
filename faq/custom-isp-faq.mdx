  <Accordion title="**What is the difference between Standard and Custom  ISP Proxies?**">
    - Standard  ISP Proxies: Offer 7-day and 30-day validity periods, support bulk discounts and carrier selection. The default bandwidth is 10M for the US and 5M for other regions, making them suitable for standard residential proxy needs.
    - Custom ISP Proxies: Exclusively dedicated to a single user during the validity period. They support specific subnet selection and on-demand bandwidth upgrades. The default bandwidth is 15M for the US and 10M for other regions, and they are optimized for business scenarios such as TikTok, Disney+, Netflix, YouTube, Amazon Prime, Reddit, ChatGPT, and more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title=" **What are the bandwidth advantages of Custom ISP Proxies?**">
    Custom ISP Proxies offer higher default dedicated bandwidth, supporting configurations of up to 35Mbps. They can also be upgraded on demand to meet business requirements for high bandwidth and concurrent multi-tasking.
  </Accordion>

Selecting your target business scenario helps us understand your specific use case, allowing us to provide you with better-matched IP resources and configuration plans.

  <Accordion title="**Does IPHyper support the UDP protocol?**">
    Custom ISP Proxies support the UDP protocol by default. This fulfills the connection requirements for UDP-based applications and services, such as media streaming, real-time communication, and other latency-sensitive scenarios.
