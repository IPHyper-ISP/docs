title: "IPHyper Buying Guide"
---

This guide walks you through ordering **Standard ISP Proxies** step by step, from choosing the IP type to completing payment. Just follow the interface, and you can have a usable IP within minutes.

The **Standard ISP Proxies** are long-term fixed IP with friendly pricing and strong value for money. It suits everyday cross-border store operations, account management, and other general business scenarios, with prices starting at \$/month.

## **Step 1: Open the Ordering Page**

After signing in to the dashboard, find **Standard ISP Proxies** in the sidebar and click to enter. You can also click the **Standard ISP Proxies** card in the top menu of the website homepage to go straight to the product page.

## **Step 2: Select IP Type**

IPHyper offers two IP types. Decide which one you need before ordering:

- Native: comes from the local user network of a real residential broadband line. The genuine origin and higher stability make it suitable for scenarios with strict account security requirements.
- Broadcast: a residential IP allocated from server networks. The lower cost makes it suitable when budget comes first and stability requirements are relatively relaxed.

## **Step 3: Select Region and ISP**

Choose the IP location. You can filter pricing by Country/Region and ISP. Note that Standard Static Residential IPs do not support custom subnet selection; subnets are assigned automatically by the system.

## **Step 4: Select Duration and Quantity**

Set the quantity and the IP duration, which can be 7 days or 30 days. The more you buy, the higher the volume discount (see the discount table above). The summary panel on the right shows the discounted total in real time.

## **Finally, Confirm the Order and Pay**

Review the order summary, make sure the region, quantity, duration, and total are correct, then choose a payment method and complete payment.

Once payment succeeds, the IP is assigned to your account. You can view it in the IP list and start using it.
