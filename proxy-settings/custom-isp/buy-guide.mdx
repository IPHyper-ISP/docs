This guide walks you through ordering a **Custom ISP Proxies** step by step, from choosing the IP type to completing payment. Just follow the interface, and you can have a usable IP within minutes.

The **Custom ISP Proxies** is a premium dedicated static IP with configurable bandwidth, built for business scenarios that demand higher network performance, with prices starting at \$3.2/month.

## Step 1: Open the Ordering Page 

After signing in to the dashboard, find “Static Residential IP” in the sidebar and click to enter. You can also click “Buy Now” under the “**Custom ISP Proxies**” card on the website homepage. 

## **Step 2: Select the IP Type**

IPHyper Custom ISP Proxies, only native IPs are available.

Native IPs originate from real residential broadband end-user networks, featuring authentic origins and superior stability, ideal for scenarios with high account security requirements.

## **Step 3: Select Region, ISP, and Subnet**

Choose the IP location with region-level filtering, and view pricing by Country/Region and ISP. Unlike Standard ISP Proxies, Custom ISP Proxies offer two subnet selection options:

- Random subnet: assigned automatically by the system, so checkout is faster.
- Selected subnet: specify a narrower subnet, for scenarios with specific requirements on IP ranges.

The summary panel on the right shows the selected region, quantity, and total in real time, so you can check them at any time. 

## **Step 4: Selecy the Bandwidth Configuration**

This is the main difference from the Standard plan. Choose a 15-35 Mbps bandwidth specification based on the concurrency level of your business; bandwidth goes up to 35 Mbps. Dedicated high bandwidth handles high-concurrency access with ease. If you are unsure, start with your estimated peak concurrency and adjust later as needed. 

## **Step 5: Confirm Duration and Quantity**

Set the quantity and the IP duration (30 days). The **Custom ISP Proxies** does not support volume discounts. The summary panel updates the total as the quantity changes.

## **Finally, Confirm the Order and Pay**

Check the order summary and make sure the business scenario, region, bandwidth, quantity, carrier, duration, and total are correct, then choose a payment method and complete payment.Once payment succeeds, the custom IP is assigned to your account. You can view and use it in the IP list.
