In this section we cover Account and Billing, which includes account information, password changes, and billing records. With this information you can track account status, manage orders, and get help with ease. The details follow below.

## **Change Password**

Updating your password regularly helps protect your account and your assets. This article shows how to change your account password in the console with email verification. 

## **Prerequisites **

- You have registered and signed in to a console account.
- Your registered email address can receive mail normally (the page shows the **Email verified** status).

## **Steps**

1. Sign in to the console, click **Account and Billing** in the left menu, make sure the top tab is **Security Settings and Account Information**, and find the **Change Password** section near the bottom of the page.
2. Enter your registered email in **Email Address**, click **Send Code**, then check your inbox and enter the code in the **Email Verification Code** field.
3. Enter the new password in the **New Password** field. You can click the eye icon on the right to check what you typed.
4. Click **Change Password**. Once the code is verified, the password is changed, and you use the new password from the next sign-in.

 

## **Billing Management**

This article shows how to view order records, filter orders, and handle unpaid orders in the console. 

## **Prerequisites**

- You have registered and signed in to a console account.
- Your account already has plan purchase records.

## **Steps**

1. Sign in to the console, click **Account and Billing** in the left menu, and switch to the **Billing Records** tab.
2. Enter an order number in the search box to locate an order, or use the **Plan Type** dropdown to filter by All / Static ISP Proxies / Custom ISP Proxies.
3. In the order list, view the plan name, order amount, amount paid, payment method, payment time, and order status.
4. For an unpaid order, click **Pay** to complete payment.
