This guide walks you through the fastest way to set up your first IP in just four steps.

## **Step 1: Register an Account**

Go to the IPHyper website, click Sign Up, and enter your email and other details to create an account. Once registration succeeds, you are taken to the console automatically.

## **Step 2: Choose a Plan**

After signing in, open the product page and choose the plan that fits you from the three product lines: **Standard ISP Proxies**, **Custom ISP Proxies**, and **Streaming Static IP**. Then click **Buy Now**.

## **Step 3: Get Your IP**

Once the order is paid, open the console and find the assigned IP address and port in the IP list.

## **Step 4: Configure and Use**

Configure the IP address and port in your browser, store tools, or code.
